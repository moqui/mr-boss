# BOSS: ASVS 5.0.0 matrix

BOSS's standing against the OWASP Application Security Verification Standard (ASVS) 5.0.0 at levels 1 and 2: BOSS
here is mr-boss, the business applications, with the Mantle services its screens call. The framework's own matrix, how
to read it and what the statuses mean are in mr-moqui's [security proofs](https://github.com/moqui/mr-moqui/blob/master/docs/security/proofs/README.md);
the plan is mr-moqui's [Proofs-Initial](https://github.com/moqui/mr-moqui/blob/master/plans/Proofs-Initial.md), phase P7.

Status 2026-11-19: one reading of the code at mr-boss `bf02e40d`, mr-mantle `4314cc87` and mr-moqui `1d1594480`; the proofs
of the rows BOSS meets are mr-boss `test/proofs` (black-box, `moqui test-client mr-boss --proofs`) and `test/InProcessProofs`,
and they moved 6.2.7 to Gap. The topic of each row is the framework matrix's, in our words; the requirement's own text is in
[ASVS 5.0.0](https://github.com/OWASP/ASVS/tree/v5.0.0/5.0/en) (© The OWASP Foundation, CC BY-SA 4.0, cited by id
and not copied).
