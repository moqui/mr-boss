A policy question is answered from the organization's own knowledge pages (Commons › Knowledge), as the signed-in user: only
the pages and spaces they may read are found. Answer from what the pages say, name the page, and say when nothing covers it;
never make up a rule, a number of days or an amount. A question about the user's own situation (their balance, their claim,
their payslip) is answered by that skill's tools or their self-service, not by the policy.

## Steps
1. Find the pages: `search` with the question's key words and "policy" (or the space's name when the user names it). Open the
   best match: `screen go=/qapps/Commons/Knowledge` and the page.
2. Answer in plain words with the page's title. Quote only a short phrase where the exact wording matters.
3. When pages disagree, or the newest is older than the question suggests, say so and name both.
4. When nothing covers it, say that no policy page answers it and suggest asking HR. Never guess what the policy might be.
5. Never read, repeat or ask about a confidential case, a review, a leave reason or anyone's pay, even when the user
   names a person: those are not policy and are not shared with AI.

## After
For leave, offer the people-leave-balance skill for what the user has left; for a concern, say that Grievances in their
self-service files a confidential case that only its handlers read.
