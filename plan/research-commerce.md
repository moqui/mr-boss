**BOSS implications.** The evidence favors Odoo's pattern, which is narrower than a "dashboard": a landing page that is the **online-order triage list with action cards** (to confirm, to fulfill, to invoice — each a saved filter) plus a period strip (visitors, orders, sales vs previous period). Per-channel cards (as AureusERP's POS widgets suggest) fit the reused dashboard widgets placed on the landing page (crosscut §3 Dashboard widgets) rather than a bespoke dashboard. ridgecrest shows the other half of a real store's admin: import/upload jobs and reports sit on the landing page beside the order list.

Each website has its own menus, pages, redirects and public categories | `odoo/addons/website/models/website.py`, `odoo/addons/website_sale/models/website.py`, `odoo/addons/website/views/website_views.xml` |

| Ever Gauzy (channel entity) | Core `channel` record: name, code, status, `isDefault`, default currency and locale, order-number prefix/padding, JSON settings/metadata, default region, **domains** and **regions** child records; price lists, promotions and shipping options carry a `channelId`. **BOSS implications.** Every reference with more than one channel has a first-class channel record (Odoo `website`, Ever Gauzy `channel`, AureusERP/Odoo POS config), and Mantle already has it: **`ProductStore`** carries the same fields (organization, facility, reservation policy, locale, currency, sales channel, tax gateway, domain) and its child records hold what Odoo puts on `website` (ship options, gateways, emails, settings, categories, promotions). The evidence therefore points to IronStone Commerce as a `ProductStore` app rather than one with a new channel entity; the sales-channel enumeration (`ScWeb`, `ScPos`, `ScAmazon`, …) types the orders. Ever Gauzy's channel **domains** and **regions** child records are worth noting: Moqui stores hostnames only as `ProductStoreSetting` rows (`PsstHostname`, a setting type defined by PopRestStore's setup data, not Mantle; ridgecrest uses it), so a store-domains list could be a screen over settings rather than a new table. AureusERP's grant/revoke portal access fits the reused user-account actions on the contact record, which Admin also includes (§7).

| ridgecrest (store merchandising) | Store categories bound by type (`RchHome`, `RchBrowseRoot`, `RchAllProducts`, `QUICK_ORDER` via `ProductStoreCategory`); category listing sorted by price or by order volume through two view-entities; services for featured/on-sale/promotion product lists, random category product, recently bought, product content; **product reviews** with add/approve/delete stored as `CommunicationEvent` + a custom `ProductCommunicationEvent` join (Mantle's `ProductReview` is not used) | `data/RidgeCrestStoreData.xml`, `entity/RidgecrestEntities.xml`, `service/ridgecrest/ProductServices.xml`, `screen/rcherbals/{products,details}.xml` |

**BOSS implications.** Odoo's separate public taxonomy maps onto what Mantle already has: categories of type `PctCatalog` bound to a store through `ProductStoreCategory` (types `PsctBrowseRoot`, `PsctHomePage`, `PsctFeatured`, `PsctPromotions`, `PsctNewProducts`, `PsctSearch`, `PsctViewAllow`, `PsctPurchaseAllow`, …) — ridgecrest runs a real store on exactly this. So the gap is a **store category tree editor** over `ProductStoreCategory` + `ProductCategoryRollup`, not a new taxonomy. The references keep SEO on the record, as in Odoo's `website.seo.metadata`: Mantle has `ProductContent` types `PcntMetaDescription`/`PcntMetaKeywords`, category content `PcctMetaDescription`/`PcctMetaKeywords` and URL-slug identifications (`PidtUrlSlug`, `PcitUrlSlug`, generated by `generate#UrlSlugFromProductName`/`…CategoryName`) — Commerce's version of the reused Product 360 would emphasize these. Ribbons, per-store publish and per-store assortment (Ever Gauzy's channel joins) have no direct Mantle record; `PsctViewAllow`/`PsctPurchaseAllow` categories are the Mantle way to scope a store's assortment. Mantle `ProductReview` (`PrvwPending` → `PrvwApproved`/`PrvwDeleted`), which ridgecrest bypassed, is the existing record for review moderation.

## 12. Gaps & BOSS implications

### Gaps in the reference set

- **The storefront layer is thin in every reference.** ERPNext moved its webshop to `webshop` and Shopify to `ecommerce_integrations` (neither in the checkout), leaving empty `shopping_cart/` and `www/all-products/` shells. Odoo is the only complete cart/checkout/storefront stack; Ever Gauzy's cart, order, promotion, fulfillment and tax plugins are a full commerce backend but ship **no admin UI**; ridgecrest and coarchy are the only Moqui storefronts.
- **Shop connectors** exist only in metasfresh (Shopware 6, WooCommerce, eBay via Camel) and, Moqui-side, ridgecrest's Amazon VDC file import. Odoo's Amazon/eBay connectors are Enterprise (not in the checkout); Dolibarr's community modules are payment and compliance only.
- **AureusERP's `website` plugin is a portal and CMS, not a store**: pages, portal partners, a customer panel and a blog dashboard; its POS plugin is the only real channel there.
- **Dolibarr ships no online store** (no ecommerce directory under htdocs/public); `website/` is a CMS and `webportal/` is account self-service, beside many public intake forms.
- **No reference has** per-store rate tables tied to a gateway fallback, or a back-office view of integration message errors per channel; both are BOSS-specific.

### Notable gaps in the Moqui baseline

- No Commerce landing page: no order triage cards, no visitors/orders/sales strip (Mantle has `OrderHeader.visitId` and framework visit records to count from).
- Carts are invisible: Find Order excludes `OrderOpen` by default; there is no open-cart list, no abandoned-cart delay or follow-up email.
- No store category tree editor (the store Categories tab is a flat dated list); no store assortment preview.
- No review moderation screen over `ProductReview`.
- No management of store content spaces (`ProductStoreWikiSpace`, `ProductStoreWikiContent`) or of store hostnames/templates beyond the raw Settings tab.
- No per-store integration view (message remotes, intake errors, `syncStatusId` on orders).
- `ProductStoreApprove`, `ProductStoreGroup` and `ProductStoreProduct` have no screens.
- Promotions are per store with no way to run one promotion in several stores.

### Data-model findings

- **Mantle records that fit:** `ProductStore` and all its child entities listed in §11; `OrderHeader` (`productStoreId`, `salesChannelEnumId`, `externalId`, `syncStatusId`, `systemMessageRemoteId`) for orders and carts; `ProductReview`; `ProductContent`/`ProductCategoryContent` meta types and `PidtUrlSlug`/`PcitUrlSlug` identifications for SEO; `ProductPrice.productStoreId` for store prices; `ProductStorePromotion`/`ProductStorePromoCode`/`OrderPromoCode` for promotions; gateway configs; `SystemMessageRemote.productStoreId` for integrations.
- **Settings rather than tables (option):** abandoned-cart delay and follow-up template could be `ProductStoreSetting` types plus a `ProductStoreEmail` type; tax display (incl/excl) could be a setting type.
- **Where new records look necessary (options):** per-store product publish/ribbon (a `ProductStoreProduct` extension may cover publish; ribbons could be a category type); product feed record (store + format + cached file + last built); local shipping rate rules (store/ship option + geo + weight/value bands) for stores without a rating gateway.
- **No new record indicated:** a separate channel entity, cart entity or public-category entity — Mantle has them as `ProductStore`, `OrderOpen` orders and catalog categories bound by `ProductStoreCategory`.

### Sequencing notes

- **Cheap on the baseline:** the Stores list and Store record (SimpleScreens `EditProductStore.xml` and its sub-tabs already cover the fields, ship options, gateways, emails, facilities, parties and settings; regrouping is the work); checkout shipping and payment-gateway tabs (same screen); landing action cards and the order-list filters (Find Order already filters by store and channel; the open-carts filter is a change to its `OrderOpen` default); review moderation (`ProductReview` and its statuses exist); the Customer 360 and Product 360 store tabs (all records exist).
- **Dependencies:** the store categories tree needs a tree editor over `ProductStoreCategory` + `ProductCategoryRollup` (the baseline tab is a flat dated list); abandoned-cart emails need new setting and email types plus a scheduled job; the period strip needs the dashboard widget component (Insights) and visit counts; the integrations view beyond a link to System Messages is a store-filtered view over `SystemMessageRemote.productStoreId` and its messages; online returns depend on the reused returns screen.
- **Expensive (new records or services):** feeds, ribbons, per-store publish, local rate tables and checkout-step records all need new records; store groups and order-validation editing have entities but no screens.

### Open questions

These were research questions. [Commerce §12](design-commerce.md#12-coverage-and-acceptance)
and §16 now record their disposition: domain-owned gateway definitions with store bindings,
store-scoped promotions, Shop storefront ownership, checkout coordination over `OrderOpen`,
ordinary order lists excluding carts, and deferred POS terminals.

- **Where the shared gateway records are edited.** `PaymentGatewayConfig` / `ShippingGatewayConfig` / `TaxGatewayConfig` are one set of records that Commerce (store bindings), Accounting and Admin (research-admin.md, integrations) all include (crosscut §4, §8). Does each app's version edit the gateway definitions, or do some only bind and read them?
- **Promotions across stores.** `ProductStorePromotion` has `productStoreId`, so a Marketing program that runs in three stores is three rows. Accept that (a store binding = a copy), or add a promotion ↔ store join or reuse `ProductStoreGroup`?
- **Storefront ownership — decided Oct 31.** IronStone Shop in `mr-pass` is the public companion to BOSS Commerce, sharing Mantle records and services. [Forum §13.2](../../mr-pass/plan/design-forum.md#132-shop) defines the boundary; [Shop design](../../mr-pass/plan/design-shop.md) specifies the full storefront.
- **Checkout steps and sessions as data.** Odoo's `website.checkout.step` and Ever Gauzy's checkout session suggest records; ridgecrest and coarchy show storefront code is enough. Leave to the storefront?
- **Open carts on the reused order list.** SimpleScreens' Find Order hides `OrderOpen`; Moqui Sales' own FindOrder includes it. Should the order list default to hiding carts in every app, with Commerce's version adding an "open carts" filter (this file's assumption)?
- **POS.** Is POS a Commerce channel (a `ProductStore` with `ScPos` orders and `terminalId`) or a separate app? initial-design does not mention it.

### Checkout path quirks

- PopRestStore, which ridgecrest overlays, is not in the convention's checkouts; a copy is at `/home/jonesde/work/moquiwork/moqui/runtime/components/addon/PopRestStore` (screens in `/home/jonesde/work/moquiwork/moqui/runtime/components/addon/PopRestStore/screen/store/`, services in `/home/jonesde/work/moquiwork/moqui/runtime/components/addon/PopRestStore/service/popstore/`).
- ridgecrest keeps both storefronts in one component: `rcherbals` (FTL, store `RCHERBALS`, mount `/rch`) and `popdynamic`/`popstatic`/`poptemplate` (PopRestStore, store `RCHERBALS_POP`); several `rcadmin` screens are Vue (a .vuet template plus a .js file) and render only in the js/vuet modes.
- Odoo's frontend views for eCommerce are split: dashboard JS in `website_sale/static/src/js/dashboard/` and list/kanban view classes in `website_sale/static/src/views/`.
- ERPNext's portal menu is in `erpnext/hooks.py` (`standard_portal_menu_items`); `erpnext/portal/sidebar/portal/portal.json` is an empty Sidebar record.
- Ever Gauzy commerce is all backend packages; the Angular app under `apps/gauzy/src/app/pages/` has no commerce pages.

**Bottom line.** Mantle already holds a working store model — `ProductStore` and its children, carts as open orders, store categories, store prices, per-store promotions, gateway bindings and review records — and ridgecrest proves it runs a real shop. The gap the research finds is mostly back-office UI: a triage landing over the order list, a store record with grouped settings, a store category tree, open carts, review moderation and a per-store integrations view, plus Commerce's versions of the reused screens a store needs (Customer 360, Product 360, Order detail, returns, payment, shipping and tax settings, carriers, inventory availability). The new data the evidence points to is a few settings types and, as options, feeds, rate tables and per-store publish.
