# Review: customer-side handoffs

Reviewer pass, Nov 1, 2026, of the Oct 31, 2026 drafts. Part A adversarially re-checked fifteen cross-app findings of the per-file reviewers; Part B walked lead to order, web order, subscription, identity and consent, and measurement across the files. Paths are relative to the mr-moqui repository root unless the report gives a shorthand; reference-app paths are under `~/work/moquiwork/3p_other/`. The coordinated decisions and dispositions are in [review-suite-apps.md](../review-suite-apps.md); this file is the reviewer's report as delivered.

1. **Summary**

- Of the 15 Part A findings, 10 are confirmed and 5 are only partly right. None is refuted outright. Two were understated:
  - R-SHP-02 is worse than reported. `invite` refuses any party that is not an organization (ExternalAccess.js:217-218). So a consumer can never get access to Billing, Sales or Support through a contact grant.
  - R-SAL-04 is worse too. The manual `approve#Order` never calls `check#OrderPreApprove`. That means Purchasing's approval hook (D8) and Sales' approval hook are both skipped when someone approves by hand.
- Several proposed fixes can be smaller or placed better:
  - **Per-hold-type release (R-SAL-04):** this already exists through `Enumeration.enumCode`. Only the seed data needs to change.
  - **Quote decline (R-SAL-03):** use the existing `cancel#Order` and its "Cancel Quote" transition. No new transition is needed.
  - **Revision (R-SAL-02):** make `orderRevision` count only commercial changes, so Purchasing's settled revision binding (D8) is fixed as well. A hash used only by Sales would fix Sales alone.
  - **Account hub (R-SHP-02):** put "My account" in PASS Account, not in Shop.
  - **Organization filters (R-SAL-09/R-MKT-06):** add a denormalized `organizationPartyId`, because entity filters cannot follow the dated role rows.
- The biggest new problem is recurring orders. Nothing marks a recurring template order, so it counts as open credit exposure and fires the approved email and stock reservation. Two designs each claim the recurring worker. Shop renewal notices clash with Dunning, which is meant to be the only scheduled reminder sender. And no step revokes access after a refund or chargeback.
- Shop has no working contract with Customer Service on any customer request. Returns, support questions and cancellations each go to their own Request or to a "shared policy" that does not exist. Customer Service never mentions Shop.
- The identity and consent steps each have two or three owners:
  - two places to merge parties, with the guard only in Sales;
  - three unsubscribe or preference pages, and `/u/` is served by BOSS, not PASS;
  - two places to file a privacy request, and Sales, Commerce, Shop and Customer Service register no privacy provider.
- Measurement has no consistent basis:
  - TrackingCodeVisit is keyed on `moqui.server.Visit`, but a remote PASS keeps its visits on the frontend.
  - Orders and revenue are defined three times, by Sales, Commerce and Marketing.
  - The cross-app funnel has no owner, even as later work.
- Below, Part A and Part B merge into 15 cross-app decisions (§5).
