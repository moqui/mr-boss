# Review: design-commerce.md and PASS design-shop.md

Reviewer pass, Nov 1, 2026, of the Oct 31, 2026 drafts. Checked against research-commerce.md, design-crosscut.md, design-forum.md, the core-app designs, design-doc-guide.md §11, active Mantle and PASS sources, the payments plan and the reference apps. Paths are relative to the mr-moqui repository root unless the report gives a shorthand; reference-app paths are under `~/work/moquiwork/3p_other/`. The coordinated decisions and dispositions are in [review-suite-apps.md](../review-suite-apps.md); this file is the reviewer's report as delivered.

### Summary

- **The designs build their own versions of things that already exist.** Shop invents its own guest, order, download and recovery tokens instead of using the shared `ExternalAccessServices` capability service (D5, crosscut §5, Forum §13.1, Sales §10). Commerce and Shop propose `PublicShopPage` when `PublicSitePage` with `get#`/`publish#SitePage` already stores version-pinned pages by role. Shop proposes new idempotency fields when `PaymentGatewayResponse.operationKey` already exists. Shop proposes a third durable delivery ledger next to Forum's PASS-DELIVERY and Marketing's MKT-SEND.
- **Shop duplicates the Customer Service Support pages, with a different model.** Shop's ReturnRequest creates a `Request`. The Support app's RequestReturn creates a `ReturnHeader` through `ReturnPolicyServices.request#CustomerReturn` (D11). Commerce's text repeats the Request model. This is the most important fix.
- **The customer gets no single account.** Shop checks ownership by "self". Billing, Support and Sales check `ExternalAccessContact` grants, which come only from an invitation ("A CRM contact relationship gives no portal access"). A shopper who registers in Shop sees nothing in Billing or Support. There are also five separate account homes (Shop, Billing, Support, Sales, Marketing).
- **Existing Mantle automation is missing from the recurring and payment-exception paths.** The native recurring loop needs base orders in OrderApproved. `clone#Order` copies payments as Promised, so `OrderPlacedAuthorizePayments` would charge every renewal by itself. Shop's coordinator does not say how it avoids a second charge. Payment and stock exceptions after placement are not D7 `OrderHold` reasons. `autoApprove#Order` already refuses to approve without authorized payment covering the total.
- **Contracts with only one side.** Insights expects Commerce's store, cart and conversion metrics, but Commerce has no Reports area. Forum expects a "manage subscription" handoff (`PublicSite.subscriptionUrl`) that Shop never sets. No file owns the editor for `ProductSubscriptionResource`/`SubscriptionResource`, which every paid-access purchase depends on.
- **A dependency is not planned.** Shop's first release includes automatic recurring billing with saved tokens. The Mantle payments plan puts subscriptions out of initial scope and Stripe later. Shop never cites that plan.
- **Both Open lists say nothing is open.** At least ten cross-app decisions below need to be added to them.
- **Coverage and names are good.** All 34 ProductStore forms are mapped. The crosscut §7.7 roll-up matches. Nearly every load-bearing Mantle name checks out.
