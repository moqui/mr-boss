## Questions for the user
1. **Base-currency posting last (P20).** Keep P20 last, adding the rule that each new money record carries its currency and a null rate from the start, and widen P20 to settlement, runs and bank lines (I-ACC-27)? Or move it to just after P8? *Recommendation:* keep it last, with the currency-carrying rule and I-ACC-05's guard row. The refusal then protects the GL, and P20 becomes a conversion change, not a schema change across 15 phases.
2. **Reversals after a lock (I-ACC-02).** When a cancelled invoice, voided payment or unapplied payment would reverse into a locked period, should Mantle date the reversal on the first open date (Odoo), or refuse the document change (ERPNext's default)? *Recommendation:* first open date behind the lock data, with the link to the original kept; refuse only under a hard lock or a closed period.
3. **Phase splits (I-ACC-14).** Split P6, P10, P17 and P18 into a/b parts, and show P2a and P2b in the table? *Recommendation:* yes. Each part gets one walk of §16.3, and the total stays about the same work.
4. **Who invites a customer or supplier contact to PASS (I-ACC-08).** Should the invitation be issued from Customer360 and Supplier360 by a Mantle service that checks the issuer's access to the party, rather than by site owners? *Recommendation:* yes. Purchasing P16 and Customer Service P15 would follow the same service.
